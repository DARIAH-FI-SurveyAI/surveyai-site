AI and LLM tools for social science data

SurveyAI builds tools for social science data — especially survey data — covering generative AI and large language models in R, and the use of AI at different steps of the survey process, with particular attention to the missing-data challenge and to the Finnish language.

Tasks & deliverables

Early-stage infrastructure work, organised around the project's own task structure.

T1

Generative AI & LLM tools for social science data

Developing and evaluating generative AI and large language model tools for analysing social science data, largely within the R environment.

T2

Multi-source analysis tools

Analysis tools for social science data drawn from multiple data sources, supporting combined and comparative analysis.

GSPBM

AI across the survey process

Mapping where AI can support the survey process, following the Generic Statistical Business Process Model (GSPBM) used in official statistics production — with particular focus on the missing-data challenge.

KIT

Safety kit for social statistics users

An infrastructure-level deliverable: practical guidance and safeguards for people working with social statistics in an AI-assisted workflow. Forthcoming guidance will e.g. review optimal AI techniques and tools.
