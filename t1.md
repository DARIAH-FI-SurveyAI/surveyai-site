Developing and evaluating generative AI and large language model tools for
analysing social science data, largely within the R environment.

## Background

This project studies how AI and LLMs can be used systematically to support
better survey quality to social science data analysis. The use of AI and LLMs 
have been tested for various tasks in survey process. Survey research has for
long faced severe challenges on missing data due to non-response. Regardless 
of advances in methodologies and survey designs the problem of missing data 
remains a key questions on the reliability of surveys. Another impactful 
challenge for social surveys is the extent of structural and thematic changes
which require both comparison over time to monitor the impact of change but
also changes in the content of surveys to respond to the relevance criteria.


## Approach

Methods developed include AI and large language models that best support the
quality improvement in the survey process, with the aim to reach high quality
standards of official statistics. Suitability of methods will be tested for 
their performance over time, reproducibility, reponsibility and ethics in 
social survey context.
